# October 24 product and judging-evidence review

This is an agent's technical product review. It is not a predicted judge score, user study, claim of market novelty or entrant-authored submission description.

## Highest-impact findings and repairs

1. Conflicting policies previously produced flags but required users to mentally compare the date consequences. The return file now shows each rule, its exact policy wording, date basis and candidate dates together. Unknown first-day counting is shown as two alternatives, not a falsely precise deadline. Unsupported units and ambiguous anchors produce no calculated shortcut.
2. Source references were readable but passive. Original-document actions now focus the supplied receipt or policy and select the exact referenced line. Viewing a source leaves confirmation intact; editing it invalidates the plan and disables stale shortcuts.
3. Selecting a rule required retyping supported fields. An explicit Review this rule action fills the anchor/basis/day count only when evidence supports them. Unknown counting, item eligibility, rationale and final confirmation remain the user's decision. No calendar is unlocked by the shortcut.
4. A line combining return terms and refund-processing terms could lose the actual return rule. Clause-level filtering preserves the return rule and its full original line, while retaining the refund-processing warning. Duplicate identical evidence remains visible without inventing a conflicting anchor.
5. The prior demo spent too long reaching the distinctive behavior. The new technical capture opens with competing rules, demonstrates original-line selection and an explicit interpretation, then proves downloads, drafts, persistence, acknowledgment withdrawal and stale-source reopening. No pitch, narration or personal reflection is generated.

## Official judging criteria and evidence gaps

The official rules list Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea and Presentation with equal weighting. Planning files are also evidence for the initial eligibility stage. Source: [official rules](https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/rules), checked October 24.

- Design: comparison, direct traceability, controlled shortcuts and recovery behavior strengthen the complete experience.
- Potential Impact: a specific consumer workflow is demonstrated. No interview, measured time-saving, independent user validation or refund success is claimed. Real entrant testing and feedback would strengthen this area.
- Innovation/Idea: the demonstrated distinction is source-linked date alternatives with an explicit confirmation gate. No “first ever” or competitor-superiority claim has been established.
- Presentation: the revised real-UI recording surfaces the distinctive behavior before routine controls. Public hosting, entrant-authored explanation and final portal completion remain separate requirements.
