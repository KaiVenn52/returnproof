# ReturnProof
A local workspace that turns pasted receipt and return-policy evidence into a reviewable return plan.

## The Unique Kernel
Every candidate deadline is attached to its exact source sentence and a visible date calculation. Unsupported or conflicting language blocks calendar export until a person supplies and confirms the applicable rule. This is evidence organization, not a declaration of legal rights.

## Who It's For
A consumer with an online purchase, a delivery date, and scattered policy text who currently copies dates into notes and risks overlooking conditions.

## The Core Loop
Paste receipt and policy → inspect extracted evidence → confirm the applicable window, date basis, and counting convention → check required materials → export a source-backed checklist and all-day calendar reminder. Return later to the saved case, or delete it.

## Inspiration & Identity
October 24 direction supersedes the initial styling. The user explicitly requested a comprehensive optimization and removal of generic AI-looking UI. Agent implementation: a return-file desk with flat original-document inputs, one paper-like result file, ink typography, burnt orange attention/actions, source line references and a local file rail. Sans-serif headings and monospaced evidence replace the previous italic-serif/teal card layout. No generated imagery. See `../brand.md`.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Personal stake not established. The delegated brief requests a useful consumer tool and a verifiable end-to-end proof of concept.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A sample receipt delivered on 2026-10-10 and a 30-calendar-day policy produce a candidate November 9 deadline when the anchor is excluded. A conflicting 14-day policy forces review. Only an explicit human confirmation unlocks calendar export. Exported text preserves the source and calculation. Reload preserves the case; delete removes it.

## The POC Boundary
English pasted text, explicit ISO dates, calendar-day rules, editable human-reviewed interpretation, source excerpts, a small local case list, checklist/JSON/ICS exports, and local deletion.

## Later
OCR, multilingual parsing, merchant-specific verified policy feeds, cross-device sync, and actual reminder delivery need separate validation.

## Explicitly Cut
No mailbox access or receipt upload: pasting avoids credential and file-handling scope. No automatic merchant messages or refund promises. No runtime LLM or remote API: deterministic narrow extraction makes unsupported language visible and needs no credential or spend. AI assistance is used in the development process required by this event.
